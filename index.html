About Me

// background · core competencies · engineering philosophy

I am Tapas Barman, an AI Engineer and Python Developer pursuing my M.Sc. in Data Science at IIIT Lucknow. My core focus centers on rigorous LLM evaluation & safety, autonomous multi-agent workflows (LangGraph), and distributed streaming data engineering (Apache Kafka/PyFlink).

My engineering background spans evaluating and aligning production LLMs with expert human feedback at Outlier (advancing mathematical reasoning and alignment), architecting automated ETL & MLOps pipelines at Aaizel International Technologies, and engineering high-accuracy OCR extraction systems at IIT Bombay.

I approach machine learning through an evaluation-first and reliability-first lens: stress-testing safety boundaries with DeepEval and Ragas, eliminating PII leakage, and engineering fault-tolerant Python backends designed to perform under high-concurrency production demands.
